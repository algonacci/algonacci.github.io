\cvsection{Selected Marketing Projects}

\textbf{Srikandi Empat Widya Utama Marketplace Growth}\hfill {\color{SecondaryText}\small May -- Jul 2024}\par
\begin{itemize}
  \item Reworked product titles, descriptions, calls to action, and storefront variants using search keywords and richer product information; one optimized listing grew from 7 to 355 views and from 1 to 13 sales.
  \item Optimized Shopee search, discovery, and store ads; July generated Rp19.6 million in ad-attributed sales from Rp1.2 million in spend, compared with Rp6.3 million from Rp3 million in June.
  \item Increased July orders from 77 to 156 and products sold from 113 to 303 while reducing ad spend by 60\%; used ad-type and keyword analysis to prioritize high-performing search campaigns.
  \item Analyzed customer geography, segmentation, purchase timing, product affinity, and reseller behavior across four publisher storefronts to recommend bundling, direct sales, live scheduling, and inventory strategies.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Kelly Florist Performance Ads and Product SEO}\hfill {\color{SecondaryText}\small Jan 2025 -- Present}\par
\begin{itemize}
  \item Developed marketplace positioning through audience profiling, competitor analysis, product SEO, catalog recommendations, and KOL or affiliate campaign concepts.
  \item Supported campaigns producing Rp67.6 million in sales from Rp6 million in ad spend by February 26, with an average ACOS of 8.86\%.
  \item Improved conversion by 1.31\% after title and description optimization and identified imported fresh-flower products as the highest-demand category.
  \item Compared bidding methods and recommended more budget for high-converting products, less exposure for high-ACOS ads, and weekly A/B testing.
\end{itemize}
